## [Apple Moves to Mitigate AI Cybersecurity Threats](https://finance.yahoo.com/video/apple-is-trying-to-address-ai-related-cybersecurity-risks-whats-going-on-202159291.html) ⭐️ 5.0/10

Apple is reportedly developing strategies to mitigate cybersecurity risks that arise from the growing use of artificial intelligence. As AI becomes deeply integrated into devices and services, new attack surfaces emerge, making it crucial for a major platform holder like Apple to protect its vast user base and maintain trust. Specific technical measures have not been disclosed, but they likely involve hardening on-device AI models, enhancing data privacy safeguards, and addressing vulnerabilities like adversarial attacks and data poisoning.

**Background**: AI-related cybersecurity risks include adversarial examples that fool models, data poisoning that corrupts training, and model extraction attacks. Apple has historically prioritized on-device processing and privacy, which can reduce cloud exposure but does not eliminate all AI-specific threats.
