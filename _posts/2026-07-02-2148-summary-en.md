## [FFmpeg 9.1 Introduces Improved AAC Encoder](https://hydrogenaudio.org/index.php/topic,129691.0.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

FFmpeg version 9.1 includes a new native AAC encoder that significantly improves audio quality, addressing long-standing issues like artifacts and poor performance compared to other encoders. This improvement makes FFmpeg a more viable tool for high-quality audio encoding, potentially reducing dependence on external encoders like Apple's Core Audio or third-party codecs, and benefits the vast number of projects and platforms that rely on FFmpeg for multimedia processing. The new encoder was primarily optimized for 48 kHz audio; while 44.1 kHz and other sample rates are supported, best quality is achieved at 48 kHz. It also includes a workaround for a bug in FFmpeg's AAC decoder related to stereo PNS (Perceptual Noise Substitution), which had gone unnoticed for years.

hackernews · ledoge · Jul 1, 14:10 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48747116)

**Background**: AAC (Advanced Audio Coding) is a widely used audio codec known for better sound quality than MP3 at similar bitrates. FFmpeg's previous native AAC encoder was known for being subpar, often producing artifacts, leading many users to rely on external encoders like fdkaac or Apple's Core Audio. The new encoder is part of the FFmpeg 9.1 release and was developed with extensive listening tests and benchmarks.
